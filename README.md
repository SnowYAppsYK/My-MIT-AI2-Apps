## 🚀 How to Explore and Use the Apps

Every application here comes in **two friendly formats** depending on what you'd like to do with it:

### 1. 📲 Just Want to Use the App? (.apk)
If you want to install and play around with the app directly on your phone:
* Download the **`.apk`** file for the app you like.
* Transfer it to your Android phone (or download it directly on your device).
* Make sure "Install from Unknown Sources" is toggled on in your settings, and you're good to go!

### 2. 🛠️ Want to Edit or Learn From It? (.aia)
If you want to see the block code, tweak the design, or build something cool on top of it:
* Download the **`.aia`** source file.
* Head over to [MIT App Inventor] and log in.
* Click on `Projects` > `Import project (.aia) from my computer` and upload the file.
